I’m Sieben, a graphic and digital media student focused on motion design, branding, video editing and visual storytelling. I like making work that feels sharp, clear and visually strong, whether that is a motion piece, a visual identity or a digital layout.

Profile

Who I am

I’m currently studying Graphic and Digital Media at AP Hogeschool in Antwerp, with a strong focus on motion design and branding. I enjoy creating visual work that is both conceptually strong and visually appealing, combining storytelling with clean design.

I work with tools like After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, DaVinci Resolve, Cinema 4D, InDesign and VS Code. My skillset includes 2D motion graphics, video editing, VFX, color grading, and HTML/CSS. I also pay a lot of attention to page hierarchy and overall visual attractiveness to make sure designs communicate clearly.

Throughout my studies, I’ve worked on a range of projects including a leporello for FOMU, a sneaker video inspired by the Nike Air Force 1, an affiliate car review for the SEAT Ibiza, a carousel for the Belgian Red Devils, and concept work like Eastpak x Juice WRLD, Bliss Boost, Winter Wonderland, and an AR sports watch.

I would describe myself as a flexible and detail-focused creator who learns quickly and works efficiently. I prefer to let the work speak for itself, but I’m also easy to collaborate with and always focused on delivering strong results for the client.
