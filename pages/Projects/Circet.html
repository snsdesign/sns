Project

Circet - Explainer Videos.

For this project, I created a series of animated explainer videos for Circet, focusing on communicating internal information in a clear, engaging and visually appealing way.

I combined custom illustrations, motion graphics and animation with voice-overs and subtitles to transform complex topics into accessible visual stories. Throughout the process, I focused on maintaining a consistent visual style aligned with Circet's corporate identity, while keeping the animations dynamic and easy to follow.

This project allowed me to further develop my skills in 2D motion design, visual storytelling and corporate communication, while gaining experience creating professional content for a real company.
