Project

Affiliate car review.

This project was a group project at school. We had to make an affiliate car review (a review of a car that includes special links or promotions that earn the creator a commission when viewers take action, like buying or booking.) We made the review about the SEAT Ibiza.

My tasks in this project were leader of production, director, editor, VFX & color grading. More detailed, I was the one who helped the cameraman and actor on set with framing and making sure we had every take.

In postproduction I was the one who chose all the footage, made the cuts and layed the base for sound design. I also did the whole process of color grading. I made the footage go from a cold, rainy day to a beautiful summery, warm day.

My final task in this project were the VFX. I applied all the 2D motion graphics, made the plot twist at the end and created zooms.
