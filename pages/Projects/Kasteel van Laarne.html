Project

Monumentvideo "Kasteel van Laarne".

For this project, I created a short cinematic video about the Castle of Laarne, focusing on its history, architecture and atmosphere.

I combined cinematic footage with a voice-over, music, sound design and motion graphics to tell the story of the castle within a short format. During the project, I focused on shot variation, pacing and creating a balanced mix between visuals and audio.

This project allowed me to further develop my skills in filming, editing, storytelling and motion design while creating a cohesive visual story around a historical location.
