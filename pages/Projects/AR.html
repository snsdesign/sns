Project

AR sports watch.

For this project, I designed and integrated a futuristic Apple-inspired holographic interface into live-action footage. The goal was to create a realistic wearable UI that naturally emerged from a sports watch while blending seamlessly with the filmed environment.

The interface follows a minimal design language using red, white, blue and fluorescent yellow accents. Performance data such as heart rate, step count, bar charts and circular graphs were animated to create a believable fitness dashboard. Particular attention was given to timing, motion and subtle animations to make the hologram feel responsive and alive.

From motion tracking and camera tracking to rotoscoping, compositing and expressions, this project introduced me to several new visual effects techniques. Combined with sci-fi sound effects and an energetic soundtrack, the final result became a polished and immersive motion graphics piece. The biggest challenge was making the hologram feel convincing, which ultimately became the part I'm most proud of.
